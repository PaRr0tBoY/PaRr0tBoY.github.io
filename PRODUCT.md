# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors evaluating Acid / PaRr0tBoY's work, projects, tools, writing, and online presence.

## Product Purpose

A personal web hub for understanding who Acid is, what they are building, and where to explore the work. Success means a first-time visitor can form a clear impression of the person and choose a relevant path quickly.

## Positioning

The site brings together mechanical-engineering practice, browser customization, software tools, and experimental product work in one maintained personal workspace.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from the web and scan a personal homepage on desktop or mobile. The site links to project landing pages, documentation, tools, blog posts, source code, and social profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The homepage is a static HTML surface with inline CSS and JavaScript. Existing project, tool, blog, social, and GitHub profile data are the source of truth. Links and labels must remain functional. No fabricated metrics, testimonials, customers, or product claims.

## Brand Commitments

The name Acid / PaRr0tBoY and the user's requested Technical Editorial Minimalism + Tactile Motion design language are binding references for this work. The user wants a distinctive personal-brand expression rather than a generic personal homepage.

## Evidence on Hand

Current content and links are in index.html. Design references and tokens are in design/. Project routes include /blog/, /product/, /tools/, and /assets/. No additional brand assets were confirmed for the homepage.

## Product Principles

- Make the person and point of view legible immediately.
- Let real work lead the visual hierarchy.
- Keep exploration direct across projects, tools, and writing.
- Use expressive structure without sacrificing scanability or accessibility.
