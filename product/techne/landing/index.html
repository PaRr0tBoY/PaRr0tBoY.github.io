TECHNE / PRODUCT DIRECTION

工具与知识，仍在成形。Tools & knowledge, still taking shape.

Techne 是一个关于工具，知识和工程实践的实验性产品方向。它还没有被压缩成一句口号，先把问题，材料和可能的工作方式摊开。Techne is an experimental product direction around tools, knowledge and engineering practice. It has not been compressed into a slogan yet — problems, materials and possible ways of working are laid out first.

STATUS / EXPLORING

现在更重要的不是宣布一个完成品，而是确认：什么样的工具，能让复杂的事情变得更容易理解，更容易继续。What matters now is not announcing a finished product, but confirming: what kind of tools can make complex things easier to understand and easier to keep going.

03 QUESTIONS
把知识变成工作流Turn knowledge into workflows

不只保存资料，也关注资料如何在具体任务中被调用，拆解和复用。Not just saving material — how it gets invoked, decomposed and reused in concrete tasks.

让工具保持可解释Keep tools explainable

操作，输入和结果之间的关系应该清楚，界面不替使用者隐藏关键过程。The relationship between actions, inputs and results should stay clear; the UI does not hide critical processes from the user.

从真实问题开始Start from real problems

先观察正在发生的工作，再决定是否值得做成产品，而不是先堆叠功能。Observe the work happening first, then decide whether it deserves to become a product — instead of stacking features upfront.
